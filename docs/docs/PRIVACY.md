# DemoHero Privacy Policy

DemoHero records your screen, optional camera, and optional microphone on this Mac so you can edit and export a product-demo video.

Media stays on your Mac. DemoHero does not create an account, does not upload recordings, and does not operate a DemoHero cloud. Takes are stored under `Movies/DemoHero` unless you export a copy somewhere else.

Camera, microphone, and Screen Recording access are used only when you turn those features on. You can deny any of them and keep using the rest of the app.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. DemoHero reads StoreKit transactions to decide whether Pro is unlocked. Apple’s privacy policy applies to payment data.

If you contact support at [demohero@googlegroups.com](mailto:demohero@googlegroups.com), we receive the contents of that email.

This policy may be updated when the product changes. The in-app copy is the current policy.
